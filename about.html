What CleverNext is

CleverNext is an online storefront for practical home, cleaning, kitchen and everyday-care products. We bring a focused selection together in one place so you can explore options, understand what an item is for and ask questions before deciding.

Our approach

Useful over unnecessary

We focus on products with a clear everyday purpose. Product descriptions are intended to help you understand the general use of each item; ask us if you need confirmation of a specific feature or specification.

Clear before you commit

We want you to be able to ask about current availability, delivery to your location and any product details before payment. The product enquiry on this website is not itself a completed purchase or a payment checkout.

Human support

You can contact us directly through WhatsApp or the contact form on our homepage. The contact form opens WhatsApp with a message for you to review and send.

Shopping with us

Product cards currently display a listed price of ₦50,000 per item. Availability, delivery coverage, delivery fees and final order details should be confirmed with CleverNext before you pay. Never rely on unverified payment details sent by an unknown person.
