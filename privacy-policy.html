Privacy policy

This policy explains what information may be handled when you visit CleverNext or contact us, and the choices available to you.

Effective date: 22 October 2026 · Last reviewed: 22 October 2026
In short: this storefront does not currently take payment or store contact-form submissions in its own database. Submitting the homepage form opens WhatsApp so you can review and send the message yourself. Third-party services and the website host may still process technical or communication data.

1. Who we are

CleverNext operates this online storefront for everyday home, cleaning, kitchen and care products. For privacy questions, contact clevernextstore@gmail.com or message us on WhatsApp.

2. Information you choose to provide

If you use the contact form, the page asks for your name, email address, phone number and message. The form builds a WhatsApp message and opens WhatsApp for you to review and send; it does not submit the form directly to a CleverNext database. If you send the message, WhatsApp processes it and CleverNext can receive the information included in it. If you email or message us directly, we receive the information you choose to send.

Please avoid sending payment credentials, passwords, identity documents or other sensitive information in a general enquiry.

3. Technical information and service providers

When you load a website, the hosting provider and related infrastructure may process technical information such as IP address, browser/device details, requested pages, timestamps and security logs to deliver, maintain and protect the service. The site also loads web fonts from Google Fonts and may link you to WhatsApp and social-media services. Those providers may process information under their own policies when you use their services.

This policy does not assume that every provider retains the same data for the same period. Please review the relevant provider's privacy information for details about its processing.

4. How information is used

  • To reply to product, availability and delivery enquiries.
  • To communicate with you about an enquiry you initiated.
  • To maintain the website, prevent abuse and troubleshoot technical issues.
  • To meet applicable legal obligations where required.

We do not currently offer a website checkout or accept payment through this storefront. Do not send money until you have confirmed the order details and verified the payment instructions directly with CleverNext.

5. Legal grounds and your choices

Where applicable under Nigerian data-protection law, information may be handled to respond to your request, take steps you ask us to take before a potential purchase, protect the website, comply with legal duties, or on another valid legal basis. You can choose not to submit an enquiry, and you can contact us to ask about personal information you have sent directly to us.

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection to certain processing, and to raise a concern with the relevant data-protection authority. These rights may be subject to lawful exceptions.

6. Sharing and international transfers

We do not sell your contact-form submissions. Information may be handled by service providers that help operate the website or communications you choose to use, or disclosed when required by law or necessary to protect rights and security. Providers such as website hosting, Google Fonts and WhatsApp may process technical or communication data in locations outside your country. The safeguards and terms of those providers may apply.

7. Retention and security

We retain messages we receive only for as long as reasonably needed to respond, maintain relevant business records, resolve disputes or meet legal obligations. The retention of messages in WhatsApp, email, browser records or service-provider logs is also affected by the relevant provider's systems and settings. We use reasonable care but cannot promise that internet transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Children

This storefront is intended for a general shopping audience and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the storefront or its services change. The effective date at the top indicates the latest revision. Material changes will be reflected on this page.

10. Contact

WhatsApp: +234 703 682 0201
